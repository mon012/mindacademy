# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro with static output, committed to Git and deployed on Cloudflare Pages.

## Users

Parents evaluating learning programs and educational guidance for children aged approximately 4–12.

## Product Purpose

Mind Academy presents its children’s learning programs, explains its teaching approaches, publishes educational articles, and gives parents a direct path to contact the academy.

## Positioning

The academy combines play-based learning with future-facing skills and established programs including Singapore Math, language, coding, and Thai learning.

## Operating Context

Visitors compare classes, read long-form Thai educational content, watch embedded lesson and testimonial videos, follow cited research, and contact the academy through its existing channels.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve every exported URL path, including Thai paths, archives, pagination, and redirects.
- Preserve original copy, outbound links, video embeds, PDF embeds/downloads, metadata, and meaningful media.
- Remove WordPress runtime, plugin, theme, cache, and duplicate assets that the rebuilt pages do not use.
- Generate a static Astro site suitable for Cloudflare Pages.
- The exported site in `old/` is the migration source of truth.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the Mind Academy name, logo, friendly learning-focused character, established orange/yellow/blue palette, and the existing Thai/English voice. Refine the incumbent identity rather than replacing it.

## Evidence on Hand

The complete static WordPress export is stored in `old/`, including page copy, class descriptions, articles, photographs, embeds, citations, social links, metadata, and redirect pages. No new claims or testimonials may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Content fidelity is more important than cosmetic simplification.
- Parents should understand the academy and reach the relevant class or contact channel quickly.
- Long-form educational content must remain comfortable to read on mobile.
- Every retained asset must have a traceable use in generated output.
- Existing URLs remain stable through the migration.
